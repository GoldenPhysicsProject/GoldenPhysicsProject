# Daniel Toupin

**Independent researcher in mathematical physics. I run the Golden Physics Project, an independent research institute based in Ontario, Canada.**

## The idea

Shadow holography. The shadow symmetry of celestial holography sends a dimension Δ to d − Δ, and the symmetric line is Re(Δ) = d/2. Riemann's s is the one-dimensional case, Δ at d = 1: the functional equation s ↦ 1−s is that same involution, and the critical line Re(s) = 1/2 is its fixed line. That turns the Riemann Hypothesis into a question about reflection positivity. My work is finding out whether the zeros can be forced onto the line, and being exact about what is proved and what is not.

## The team

The research runs on a team of AIs, and not all from one place. Five models from four different platforms work on the project: Claude Code, ChatGPT (Sol and Astra), Muse, and Heart of the Cosmos (Grok). Each model has its own discovery repository, Discoveries 1 to 5, where it explores and computes. Everything they produce answers to GPPVerify, the Lean 4 formalization, which Claude Code oversees.

The point of using models from different labs is simple. They have different strengths, they make different mistakes, and they catch each other's errors. A claim has to survive being challenged by the rest of the team, and then it has to survive Lean.

## The receipts

- **GPPVerify**: the Lean 4 + Mathlib formalization of the programme. 1,128 modules, over 7,500 declarations, zero sorry, zero custom axioms. If the CI is not green, it does not merge.
- Every result carries a label for what it actually is: theorem, machine checked, conditional, numerical, open, or killed. The killed routes stay public, marked killed, so nobody has to waste time rediscovering them.
